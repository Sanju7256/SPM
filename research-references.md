# External reference notes — SPM Interiors Design

## Business-structure reference

Reviewed [RAK Interiors](https://www.rakinteriors.com/) only to understand the depth of a professional interior-design company experience. The public homepage includes an about/company story, transformation showcase, room/service segments, turnkey execution narrative, project/gallery links, current-news/blog articles, client testimonials and prominent contact/consultation actions. These are broad content categories only; no RAK text, claims, statistics, testimonials, layouts, imagery, logos or colors are reused. The SPM site uses its own route structure and original copy.
